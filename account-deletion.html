Delete Your Reset Account

Reset: Evening Journal · com.jpdevland.reset

Delete in the app

  1. Open Reset and go to Settings.
  2. Select Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning, confirm deletion, and wait for the completion message.

Request deletion without app access

Email johnprabu0702@gmail.com with the subject Reset account deletion request. Send the request from the email connected to the account when possible. We may ask for limited information needed to verify ownership; do not send a password, journal text, or payment-card details.

What is deleted

Completion deletes the active Reset account identity and associated profile, journal entries, reminders, and app data from the live service. The deletion cannot be undone.

What may remain

Encrypted backups may retain deleted data until normal backup rotation completes. Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or Reset may retain transaction records where required for refunds, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, or legal obligations.

Subscription notice: deleting a Reset account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in your Apple or Google subscription settings.
