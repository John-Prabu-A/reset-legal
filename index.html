Reset: Evening Journal

A softer place to put the day down.

Reset is a private journal for evening reflection and the little moments you want to remember. Write, speak, save a photo, or ask for an AI draft when it feels helpful.

A journal that fits your day

  • Keep a short guided evening reflection or a freeform Story Diary for the moments worth revisiting.
  • Add photos with your own short captions, or save a private voice memory to a diary page.
  • Use on-device reflection, or choose cloud AI after giving explicit consent. Story Diary drafts use your words and captions; photo pixels and voice recordings are not sent to the draft model.
  • Export your journal and diary as PDFs, or delete your account and associated live data from the app.

Your words stay yours

Journal entries and diary media are tied to your account and are not shared with other users or used to train our own AI models. Cloud AI receives only the content needed for the feature you request, after consent. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Reset is a journaling and general-wellness tool, not medical or crisis care. If you need urgent help, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
