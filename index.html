Reset: Evening Journal

Make every night easier to process.

Reset is a private guided journal for putting the day down. Write or speak through a short evening reflection, then choose on-device or consented cloud AI support when you want a thoughtful summary.

Android review access

This time-limited internal build is provided for Google Play merchant and app reviewers. It is not a public production release and does not enable Android checkout.

For review support, contact johnprabu0702@gmail.com. Do not send journal text, passwords, or payment details by email.

What Reset does

  • Guides a three-step evening reflection and saves entries privately to the signed-in account.
  • Supports optional voice-to-text journaling using device speech services.
  • Offers on-device reflection or cloud AI only after the person explicitly consents to cloud processing.
  • Lets people export their journal data and permanently delete their account from the app.
