Help for Reset: Evening Journal

Contact

Email johnprabu0702@gmail.com. Include your device type, app version, and a short description of the problem. Do not email journal text, passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Purchases

  • Use Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases after reinstalling or changing devices.
  • Apple and Google manage subscription cancellation and refunds. Deleting a Reset account does not cancel a store subscription.
  • Never send full payment details. A store receipt or order identifier may be requested when needed to investigate access.

Data and privacy

  • Export journal data from Settings → Export Your Data.
  • Delete the account from Settings → Delete Account.
  • See account-deletion instructions if the app is unavailable.
