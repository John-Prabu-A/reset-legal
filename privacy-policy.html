Privacy Policy

Reset: Evening Journal · App by John Prabu A (johnprabu0702@gmail.com)

Last updated: May 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Reset: Evening Journal collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application. By using Reset, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, profile data.
  • Journal entries: text, voice recordings, mood ratings, AI reflections — stored securely, never shared.
  • Usage data: anonymous analytics events to improve the product.
  • Device information: device type, OS version, crash reports.
  • Push notification token: for optional reminders you configure.
  • Subscription status: managed by App Store or Google Play — we do not store payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve journaling and AI reflection features.
  • To send optional evening and morning notifications you configure.
  • To manage subscriptions and provide support.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve stability.

We do not sell your data or use journal entries to train AI models.

3. Third-Party Sub-Processors

  • Supabase (supabase.com) — Cloud database and auth. Data stored in Singapore (ap-southeast-1).
  • OpenAI (openai.com) — AI reflection generation. Entry text sent to OpenAI API as sub-processor; prohibited from training on your data.
  • PostHog (posthog.com) — Anonymous analytics. No journal content sent.
  • Sentry (sentry.io) — Crash reporting. Device info and stack traces only, no journal content.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) — Subscription management.
  • Expo (expo.dev) — Push notification delivery.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — Payment processing.

4. Data Retention

Data retained while your account is active. Delete account via Settings → Delete Account — all data permanently deleted within 30 days. Export data via Settings → Export Your Data.

5. Security

Journal entries stored with row-level security (RLS) — only your authenticated account can access your data. Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

6. Children's Privacy

Reset is not directed at children under 13. Contact johnprabu0702@gmail.com if you believe a child has provided data.

7. Your Rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your data via Settings, or by contacting johnprabu0702@gmail.com.

8. International Transfers

Data may be processed in Singapore and the United States by the sub-processors above. We ensure adequate data protection standards.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made. Continued use means acceptance.
